Species slot table

64 slots of 0x50 bytes. A creature claims a run of consecutive slots. Amber slots are loaded and carry live data but no owner stamp — those read as free if you only test the owner byte, and loading over one clobbers a creature in use. A cyan underline marks a model this session's log shows us loading.
